Who We Are

The SAVS Association NZ (SAVS stands for Students Against Vaping & Smoking) is a student-led movement of us working to reduce vaping, smoking, cannabis and other drug use among our peers. Our "why" is to do everything we can, as youth, to make sure we, our younger peers, and the next generations have a better future than the current reality. We bring youth together to collectively advocate for change at the political level, while creating a network of passionate youth and adults across the country.

Students using vapes, cigarettes, cannabis, and less commonly, other recreational drugs in school bathrooms has been a widespread issue across New Zealand for years. This issue isn't just limited to schools; it's also common to see some youth vaping in public spaces like parks and train stations. Despite lots of adults trying their best, we're still seeing it becoming a bigger and bigger problem.

What We Do

Every big movement starts small. In each school, the Student Council plans the next steps Members can take to help their school and local community in this context. This could include many things. One example is creating anti-vaping posters for school buildings with the school's permission, while being mindful and balancing the risks of the "pink elephant" effect. They would show the risks of smoking and vaping, and how overseas vape companies specifically target youth for profit, etc. A key thing to note is that SAVS is not focused on immediately reporting (or "snitching on") every student who vapes. That creates a wellbeing and safety risk for students, such as bullying. All of this, however, is just the start. Our goal is to push for change at the NATIONWIDE level, e.g. petitions/bills. We aim to help reduce the issue as a whole.

Building a Community – We also want to gather community support and create a network of thousands behind the cause. The number of Supporters, along with signatures for the potential petition(s), would show the government that there are lots of people supporting what we're advocating for. We hope to partner with like-minded groups and spread to more schools. Anyone can be a Supporter, including people from outside our participating schools. The more Supporters we have, the stronger our voice will be.

How Our Organization Started

Eknoor Singh, the National Director, founded the Association around mid to late 2026 at the age of 16. From a very young age, he developed a lot of hatred and disgust for harmful substances like cigarettes and vapes, especially after understanding how much damage they could do. He had a desire of doing something later in life to fight the problem and advocate for positive change.

How It Is Structured

A SAVS Student Council in every school – Each participating school has its own “SAVS Student Council” of around 15 to 30 Members. Each council holds its own meetings and works semi-independently. It grows support within its school and takes the steps it can to influence change there, while sitting under the umbrella of the Association and contributing to it.

The SAVS Caucus – Alongside Members' in-school meetings, which they hold at their own convenience, each school sends one representative (usually the school’s SAVS Leader) to The SAVS Caucus. The main central body of the Association, which is what we call "The SAVS Caucus", is where, for example, our petition(s) is drafted and finalised, and where change beyond the school gates is led.

Members and Supporters – Members are the students in the SAVS Student Council, who commit to organising, promoting, and running/attending the in-school meetings and other events. They also have leadership opportunities, though which might be competitive. Supporters are students/youth, teachers, and even adults in general, who just support the initiative by, for example, signing the petition(s) when it comes out, promoting it, etc. They usually don't have to commit to anything. Regardless, both Members and Supporters are part of the SAVS Community.
